METROSCAN AI - PROTOTYPE OVERVIEW
================================================================================

This platform is designed to automate the enforcement of the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules by analyzing product images (both from the field and from e-commerce) and flagging violations.

Currently, it operates entirely on the frontend using a robust mock dataset to simulate the AI/Backend processing.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Login & Role Simulation (/login)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The entry point of the app simulates Role-Based Access Control (RBAC).
* Features: You can log in as an Inspector (field officer), Supervisor (HQ approval), or System Admin (configuration).
* How it works: Selecting a role saves it to your browser's local storage and dynamically adjusts what capabilities you have access to later on (like the capability matrix in Settings).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. Executive Command / Dashboard (/dashboard)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This is the high-level operational overview for supervisors and admins to monitor platform health and enforcement metrics.
* Features:
  - KPI Row: Real-time stats on Overall Compliance %, items needing review, and total violations.
  - Trend Chart: A visual comparison of Total Scans vs. Violations over a selected time period.
  - Category Breakdown: Shows which product categories (e.g., Food & Grocery vs. Imported Goods) have the highest compliance rates.
  - Top Violations: Ranks the most common infractions (e.g., "Non-standard unit").
  - Urgent Alerts: Automatically surfaces the most severe active violation so it can be actioned immediately.
* How it works: It dynamically calculates all of these numbers on the fly by reading the mock database.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. Bulk Ingestion (/bulk-upload)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Designed for scraping e-commerce platforms or processing large batches of images from a desktop.
* Features: Features a drag-and-drop zone for ZIP/CSV files. Once dropped, it simulates a live progress bar (Parsing -> Queuing -> Rule Engine).
* How it works: Below the upload zone, it displays a real-time feed of processed items, immediately categorizing them as "Compliant", "Review", or "Flagged" based on the simulated AI extraction.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. Field Dossiers / Repository (/repository)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The central database of all historical and active scans.
* Features: A searchable, filterable grid of all product dossiers. It displays product thumbnails (with badges showing how many evidence photos are attached), docket IDs, and the overall status.
* How it works: You can search by brand, docket ID, or filter by status. Clicking any card opens the deep-dive Inspection Detail view.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. Single Scan (/scan)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The mobile-first tool for Field Inspectors physically looking at products in stores.
* Features:
  - Native Camera Launch: Uses standard browser APIs to open the phone's rear camera directly.
  - Smart Framing & Quality Gate: Displays a framing overlay to guide the officer. Before accepting the photo, it runs client-side AI (Canvas API) to check if the image is too blurry (Laplacian variance) or too dark (Luminance).
* How it works: If the photo is blurry, it warns the user to retake it. If the user clicks "Use Anyway," it simulates passing a low-confidence flag to the backend, resulting in a "Needs Officer Review" status.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. Inspection Detail / Dossier View (/inspection/[id])
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The core "Engine Room" of the application where an individual product is judged against the law.
* Features:
  - Bounding Box Viewer: The product image is overlaid with interactive boxes. Green = Good, Yellow = Suspect, Red = Violation. Hovering over a box reveals details (e.g., the exact font-size ratio to see if text is too small to read).
  - Evidence Gallery: A scrollable strip of raw evidentiary photos, secured with simulated SHA-256 cryptographic hashes for legal chain-of-custody.
  - Extracted Metadata: A clean table showing what the OCR read (Brand, Net Quantity, MRP).
  - Statutory Checklist: Evaluates the metadata against specific laws (e.g., Rule 12 demands "1 kg", not "1000 gms"; Rule 6 demands MRP includes all taxes).
  - Actions: Buttons to generate and download PDF/DOCX legal memos or issue notices.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. Regional Analytics (/analytics)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A deep-dive visualization screen for identifying systemic issues.
* Features: Shows state-wise non-compliance percentages (e.g., Maharashtra at 18.2%), average resolution times, and a comparative chart mapping which specific rules (Rule 6 vs. Rule 12) are being violated the most over time.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. System Settings (/settings)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Configuration and preferences.
* Features: Allows the user to edit their profile details, set notification preferences (e.g., Daily Digests, High-Severity Emails), and view a read-only Capability Matrix that confirms what their current Role is legally allowed to do in the system.

================================================================================
The Visual Theme:
The entire application was built on a custom 12-column grid system using shared components. The global styling tokens use a Dark Navy/Off-Black sidebar with Muted Olive/Sage Green accents, giving it a highly professional, authoritative aesthetic perfectly suited for a government compliance tool.
